# Chapter 1 – Executive KPI Dashboard

## 1. Business Context

E-commerce platforms generate large amounts of operational data, including orders, payments, deliveries, and customer reviews. Transforming these data into meaningful business insights is essential for supporting data-driven decision-making.

This project analyzes the overall performance of the Olist marketplace through a series of business-oriented analyses covering sales, products, customers, delivery performance, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 2. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to evaluate the business performance of the Olist marketplace by applying SQL and Python to answer key business questions.

Rather than focusing only on technical implementation, the project emphasizes how data can be used to identify business trends, investigate operational performance, and support further business analysis.

---

## 3. Executive KPIs

The project uses six key performance indicators (KPIs) to provide an overview of the company's overall performance:

### Total Revenue

Measures the total value of customer payments recorded in the dataset.

### Total Orders

Measures the total number of orders recorded in the marketplace.

### Total Customers

Measures the number of unique customers using `customer_unique_id`.

### Average Order Value (AOV)

Measures the average customer payment value per order.

### Delivery Rate

Measures the percentage of orders successfully delivered to customers.

### Average Review Score

Measures the overall level of customer satisfaction based on review scores.

These KPIs provide a high-level view of business performance and serve as the starting point for the detailed analyses in the following chapters.

---

## 4. Project Structure

The following chapters explore the business from different perspectives:

- **Chapter 2 – Sales Performance:** Revenue, order volume, and Average Order Value
- **Chapter 3 – Product Analysis:** Product sales and order growth by product category
- **Chapter 4 – Customer Analysis:** Customer growth and purchase frequency
- **Chapter 5 – Delivery Performance:** Delivery time and on-time delivery performance
- **Chapter 6 – Customer Satisfaction:** Review scores, delivery performance, and low-score review analysis
- **Chapter 7 – Overall Summary & Business Conclusion:** Overall findings, business implications, limitations, and areas for further analysis
