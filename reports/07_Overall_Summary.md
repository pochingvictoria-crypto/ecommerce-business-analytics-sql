# Chapter 7 – Overall Summary & Business Conclusion

## 7.1 Overall Business Performance

The analysis indicates that the Olist marketplace experienced substantial business growth during the observed period.

Revenue and completed order volume increased significantly, while Average Order Value remained relatively stable. This indicates that sales growth was driven primarily by increasing transaction volume rather than substantially higher spending per order.

The customer base also expanded considerably. Between January–August 2017 and the same period in 2018, the number of unique customers increased from **21,404 to 51,612**, representing approximately **141.1% growth**.

Overall, the business expanded primarily through higher customer and transaction volumes.

---

## 7.2 Key Business Findings

### 1. Business growth was primarily volume-driven

Revenue and completed orders increased substantially, while Average Order Value remained relatively stable.

This suggests that business growth was primarily associated with processing more transactions rather than generating substantially higher spending from each order.

### 2. Multiple major product categories contributed to growth

Product sales growth was distributed across several major categories.

**Health & Beauty** recorded the largest absolute increase in product sales, while **Watches & Gifts, Sports & Leisure, Computers & Accessories**, and other major categories also contributed substantially.

The similar increases in product sales and completed orders indicate that category growth was largely associated with increasing transaction volume.

### 3. Customer-base expansion was substantial, while repeat purchasing remained limited

The number of unique customers increased from **21,404 to 51,612** between the comparable January–August periods.

However, across the available dataset, only approximately **2.99%** of customers completed two or more purchases.

This indicates that customer-base expansion was an important component of business growth, while repeat purchasing remained limited during the observed dataset period.

### 4. Delivery speed remained stable, but reliability weakened

Delivered order volume increased from **21,997 to 52,777**, while average delivery time remained almost unchanged at approximately **12 days**.

However, the on-time delivery rate declined from **95.82% to 90.63%**.

This indicates that the business maintained average delivery speed while experiencing a decline in its ability to consistently meet estimated delivery dates.

### 5. Customer satisfaction remained relatively high, but negative experiences became more frequent

Average review score declined slightly from **4.23 to 4.14**, while the proportion of 1-star reviews increased from **7.6% to 10.4%**.

Late deliveries were associated with substantially lower review scores, averaging **2.59**, compared with **4.30** for on-time deliveries.

Low-score review comments also frequently contained delivery- and fulfillment-related expressions such as `nao recebi` ("did not receive"), `nao entregue` ("not delivered"), and `nao chegou` ("did not arrive").

---

## 7.3 Business Conclusion

Overall, the Olist marketplace achieved substantial growth through expansion in customer and transaction volumes across multiple major product categories.

At the same time, the analysis identifies several areas that became increasingly relevant during the growth period. Repeat purchasing remained limited, while delivery reliability weakened despite stable average delivery times. Customer review analysis also showed that late deliveries were associated with lower satisfaction and that delivery- and fulfillment-related issues appeared frequently in low-score comments.

Taken together, these findings suggest that future analysis should examine not only continued sales and customer growth, but also the factors associated with **repeat purchasing, delivery reliability, and customer experience**.

The available data does not establish that business growth directly caused the observed changes in delivery performance or customer satisfaction. Instead, these results identify areas where additional operational and customer-level analysis may provide further business insight.

---

## 7.4 Limitations & Further Analysis

Several limitations should be considered when interpreting the results.

- The dataset does not include product listing dates, marketing activities, promotions, website traffic, or customer acquisition channels. Therefore, the underlying drivers of customer and order growth cannot be directly identified.
- Product-level sales were calculated using item prices because order-level payment values cannot be uniquely assigned to individual product categories in multi-item orders.
- The low repeat-purchase rate should be interpreted within the available observation period and should not be treated as a direct measure of long-term customer retention.
- The relationship between delivery performance and review scores is observational. Although late deliveries were associated with substantially lower review scores, the analysis does not establish causality.
- Delivery performance may also be influenced by factors such as seller performance, customer location, logistics conditions, and estimated delivery windows.

Further analysis could therefore focus on:

- Customer cohort and repeat-purchase behavior
- Seller-level delivery performance
- Geographic differences in delivery reliability
- Product categories associated with low-score reviews
- Operational factors contributing to late deliveries
- Customer acquisition and marketing performance, if additional data becomes available
